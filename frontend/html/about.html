A Passion for Culinary Perfection

The Story Behind The Velvet Fork

Born from a shared vision to bring Michelin-inspired haute cuisine, soulful regional gravies, and transcendent hospitality to Greater Noida.

Our Genesis

Preserving Legacy, Redefining Elegance

Founded by visionary epicureans, The Velvet Fork was conceived as more than a restaurant — it was envisioned as a temple of taste. We believe that dining is an emotional journey sculpted through the symphony of hand-pounded spices, artisanal clay pot cooking, and meticulous tableside service.

From our slow-simmered 12-hour gravies and fresh burrata sourdough pizzas to cold-churned saffron smoothies, every recipe represents months of relentless experimentation, sourcing pure cold-pressed oils, organic dairy, and heirloom grains.
